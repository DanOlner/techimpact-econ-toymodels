AI, skills and jobs: building a two-sector model

A model of how efficiency-improving technology moves prices, pay and jobs, built step by step. Each stage below is kept as it was when completed, so earlier results stay available as the model grows.

  1. Stage 1: one sector, first test model

    Completed 2026-10-14

    One sector plus “everything else”, with one and then four worker types; technology that augments or automates. Research’s tests 1–10 with expected values, charts of Bessen’s inverted U, augmenting and the hand-loom case, the two presets’ base years, and the first regime maps at three values of θ.

  2. Stage 2: two sectors and the supply chain

    In progress: this page is updated as the stage is built

    Two sectors, manufacturing and design, buying from each other; two firms in each sector; six worker types. Both presets solved, with AI in design, automation in manufacturing and one firm adopting alone; who gains through pay and through prices; and checks of the supply chain, the new solver and the six types.
